# MNIST Attribution

`FutoshikiInk/Resources/HandwrittenDigitClassifier.mlmodelc` is an adapted model derived from the MNIST database of handwritten digits by Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes, and Christopher J. C. Burges. MNIST is derived from National Institute of Standards and Technology source data.

- Canonical dataset page: https://yann.lecun.org/exdb/mnist/
- NIST data terms: https://www.nist.gov/open/copyright-fair-use-and-licensing-statements-srd-data-software-and-technical-series-publications
- Adapted model license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Changes: on 2026-09-02, classes 1 through 8 were selected, a compact convolutional network was trained with limited stroke-width augmentation, and the network was converted to a float16 Core ML program. Raw images are not redistributed.

The adapted model may be shared and adapted under CC BY-SA 3.0 with appropriate credit, a link to the license, an indication of changes, and distribution of adaptations under the same license. The remainder of Futoshiki Sky is not licensed under CC BY-SA merely by aggregation with this model.

ARTWORK PROVENANCE

Read attribution and terms
Original Futoshiki Sky constellation silhouettes

Generated using built-in ImageGen for this project, 2026-09-07; selected for implementation 2026-09-08 after native v5 review. The PNGs are original generated silhouette art, not copied IAU charts, Stellarium illustrations, or historical atlas illustrations. Positional guides and registration use separately attributed astronomical data.

Selected unchanged PNGs: corona-borealis.png (B), lyra-refit.png (A), delphinus.png (A), cygnus.png (B), aquila.png (A), scorpius-graphic.png (B).

Each original is 1536 by 1024 pixels, containing A on the left and B on the right. The app selects a crop and applies the recorded registration at rendering time. The initial free-set source PNGs were integrated unchanged.

Exact prompt history and SHA-256 identity record: docs/constellations/research/artwork-provenance.json in the project source documentation.

Pro collection, selected 2026-09-08: crater.png (A), leo.png (B), capricornus-refit.png (B), perseus-production-refit.png (B), taurus.png (B), draco.png (A). These are original built-in ImageGen illustrations created for this project from fixed factual map guides and the existing original eagle style reference. Perseus received a targeted lower-leg pose refinement during production preparation; the other selected source PNGs are unchanged. No third-party constellation illustration was copied.

Pro source hashes: docs/constellations/pro-selection-v1/production-artwork.json. Original and revision prompts: PROMPTS.json, REVISION_PROMPTS.json, and PRODUCTION_ART_PROMPTS.json in that same directory. Crops and uniform illustration registrations are declared by the native catalog; they never transform individual star coordinates.
