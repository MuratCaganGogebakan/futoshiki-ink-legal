Privacy Policy

Futoshiki Ink is designed around a simple privacy idea: your puzzle work and handwriting belong on your iPad, not on our servers.

Effective August 31, 2026 · Last updated August 31, 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • No account is required.
  • Handwriting recognition happens on device.
  • We do not run advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information when purchases are enabled.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Murat Göğebakan, a sole proprietorship (şahıs şirketi) registered in Bursa, Türkiye (“Futoshiki Ink,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information in connection with the Futoshiki Ink iPad application (the “App”), this website, and customer support.

For questions or privacy requests, email gogebakan.cagan@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the App

Handwriting and puzzle information

The App processes Apple Pencil strokes, recognized digits, candidates, puzzle state, settings, and generated puzzles on your device. Handwriting recognition and puzzle generation are performed locally. We do not receive or upload your handwriting, puzzle answers, or Pencil strokes.

The App may save puzzle state and preferences in local app storage so that your experience can continue between launches. You control this data through the App and your device. Removing the App may remove locally stored app data, subject to iPadOS behavior and any device backup settings you control.

Purchase information

If in-app purchases are available, Apple processes the transaction and RevenueCat helps the App read product information, confirm entitlements, and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an automatically generated anonymous App User ID. Futoshiki Ink does not provide RevenueCat with your name or email address and does not use a custom account identifier because the App has no account system.

This information is used only to offer, complete, validate, and restore the App’s purchase. It is not used by us for advertising or tracking across apps. Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy terms. Learn more in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the email address you use, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request, diagnose an issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send handwriting samples, screenshots, or other personal information unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.

3. Information handled by this website

This is a static website. We do not add analytics, advertising pixels, tracking scripts, account systems, or first-party cookies.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and timestamps to deliver, secure, and operate the service. That processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, browsing history, or search history through the App. We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

5. When information is shared

We share information only as needed for the following limited purposes:

  • Purchases: with Apple and RevenueCat to complete, validate, and restore in-app purchases.
  • Support: with the email and infrastructure providers needed to receive and respond to your message.
  • Legal and safety: when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or respond to lawful process.
  • Business transition: if the App or business is transferred, subject to appropriate notice and applicable law.

We do not share handwriting or puzzle content because that information does not leave the App.

6. Retention

Local app data remains under the control of your device and is retained until it is cleared by the App, iPadOS, or you. Purchase information is retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies and legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain business records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations.

7. Security

Keeping puzzle and handwriting processing on device reduces the information exposed to network services. Where third-party services are needed for purchases or support, we use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

Futoshiki Ink is a general-audience puzzle app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. International processing

Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, and email providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Their privacy policies describe their safeguards and international transfer practices. Support requests sent to us may be processed in Türkiye.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, or to receive a portable copy. Because we do not operate accounts and do not receive your on-device handwriting or puzzle data, we may have no such data to identify or return. For support correspondence or other information held directly by us, email your request to gogebakan.cagan@gmail.com.

You can remove local app data by using available in-app controls or deleting the App. Purchase records and refund requests are managed through Apple; RevenueCat privacy requests may also be directed to RevenueCat under its Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide additional notice where required.
