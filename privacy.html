Privacy Policy

Updated 23 September 2026

Your handwriting and puzzle progress stay on your iPad. No account is required.

Your pages

Futoshiki Ink processes handwriting, recognition, puzzle generation and hints on your device. We do not upload your Pencil strokes or puzzle answers. Progress, settings and handwriting are saved in local app storage; device backups follow your Apple settings. Deleting the app may remove those local saves.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat helps us show products, verify lifetime access and restore purchases. It processes purchase history, an automatically generated app user identifier, and technical information such as app and operating-system versions to provide this service. We do not send it your handwriting, name or email, and we do not receive your payment-card details.

See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy for their handling of this information, including retention and international processing.

No advertising or tracking

We do not run advertising, use cross-app tracking, or sell personal information. This website has no analytics scripts or cookies added by us. Its host, GitHub Pages, processes technical request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Support and your choices

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to help with your request and keep them only as long as needed for support and legal obligations. Our email provider also processes those messages. Support is handled in Türkiye; service providers may process information in other countries.

You can manage local data through the app and your device. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to its use. Contact us about information held by us or a privacy concern, including a child’s information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. We cannot access the puzzle work stored only on your device.

Contact and updates

Futoshiki Ink is made by Murat Göğebakan, an independent developer in Türkiye. For privacy requests, email gogebakan.cagan@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date. We will give additional notice when required.
