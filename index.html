The idea

Futoshiki was made for paper. The iPad should feel just as direct.

The printed puzzle stays exact and architectural. Your handwriting stays organic and personal. The software works quietly underneath both.

01

Your ink stays ink.

Recognition adds meaning without replacing your strokes with typeset digits.

02

Intelligence stays quiet.

No interruptions while you think. Hints reveal one useful step only when you ask.

03

Your work stays private.

Handwriting recognition and puzzle generation happen on your iPad. No account is required.
