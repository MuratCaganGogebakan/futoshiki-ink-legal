Common questions

Answers, without the runaround.

My handwriting was not recognized. What should I try?

Write a full-size digit from 1 through 8 near the center of the cell, then pause briefly. Small marks near a cell’s edge are treated as candidate notes. If a reading is uncertain, your ink stays untouched—erase and rewrite, or use the hand control for touch entry.

Does my handwriting leave my iPad?

No. Pencil strokes and digit recognition stay on your device. The App does not upload handwriting, puzzle answers, or candidate notes. See the Privacy Policy for the complete explanation.

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?

Open Futoshiki Ink, tap the bookmark-shaped unlock control, then choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the iPad is signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase and is connected to the internet.

I was charged but premium is still locked.

First try Restore Purchases and restart the App. If premium remains locked, email us with the App version, your iPadOS version, and the approximate purchase time. Do not send payment-card details or your Apple Account password.

How do refunds work?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. If your purchase may be eligible, start at reportaproblem.apple.com. Refund eligibility varies by country or region and applicable consumer law.

Can I solve without Apple Pencil?

Yes. Futoshiki Ink is designed around Apple Pencil, but it includes a touch-entry alternative for accessibility and correction.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include your iPad model, iPadOS version, Futoshiki Ink version, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it. A screenshot can help, but share one only if you are comfortable with everything visible in it.
