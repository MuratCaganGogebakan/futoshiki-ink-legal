A brief description and your app version are helpful. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Restore lifetime access

Open Premium (or Lifetime access) at the top right of Home, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection is needed to restore; previously verified lifetime access can be used offline.

Your saved pages

Your handwriting and progress are stored on your iPad. Please do not delete the app to troubleshoot: doing so may remove your saved pages. Include your iPad model, iPadOS version and app version when reporting a problem.
